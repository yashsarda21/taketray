Design direction

Videos lead, chrome recedes. Clip tiles are 3:4 in a three-column grid. Reel cards lead with their cover. The tag player and clip viewer are always dark on black, whatever the theme, so footage reads well in bright sunlight.

Graphite with marigold. An almost colourless brand, like a camera app: the videos bring the colour. Graphite blue-grey is the brand and every primary action. Marigold has one job: it marks clips that are waiting (the To tag badge, the banner, the strip on the Shooting mode card), so it is the only accent on screen. Red stays for REC and real errors.

One tap per clip. In the tag player, tapping a reel tags the clip and moves on. Tagging into several reels is a visible "Pick several" mode, so the one-tap path never gets slower.

References are links, not videos. TakeTray never downloads a reference or fetches its preview, so a reference is a designed card: a striped 9:16 tile, the source app's icon from the phone, the label and an Open button.

Nothing scary about videos. Every remove or delete repeats, in a green box, that the originals stay in the gallery.

Material 3, with a brand on top. Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular numbers for durations, rounded Material Symbols, standard M3 components and motion tokens.

    TakeTray style

    The app's own design language, built on the Material 3 tokens below but not using stock Material components.

    1 · Dark-first, OLED

    Designed dark first. Dark surfaces drop one step to near-black so video glows; light theme is fully supported.

    2 · Media is the surface

    Reel covers run full-bleed with the name on the image. When Shooting mode is on, its card shows your latest clip behind a brand tint.

    3 · Floating chrome

    Navigation is a floating glass pill; sheets, dialogs, menus and toasts float with blur and hairline borders.

    4 · Pills, not boxes

    Tabs, buttons and badges are fully rounded. Containers use 20–32 dp corners; settings use inset grouped rows.

    5 · Bold, few words

    34 sp extra-bold titles with tight tracking; secondary text recedes. Every action is still labelled.

    Structure

    Navigation map

    Bottom navigation with three tabs: Reels, To tag (with a marigold count badge) and Settings. Click any box to open that screen.

    Why bottom navigation

    Reels and To tag are the two places people go all day, and both are one thumb-tap away from anywhere. Settings is rarely used, but with only two other destinations it costs nothing as a third tab, and it is where VN storage and the notification fix live. A top-bar gear would hide those.

    Rules

    • Switching tabs clears that tab's back stack; Android back from a tab root leaves the app.
    • Back closes overlays first (menu, sheet, dialog, selection mode), then goes up one screen.
    • Deep links: "new clips" and "evening reminder" notifications → To tag. "VN copies" reminder → reel detail with the Done editing dialog. "Still shooting?" → Turn off works from the shade.
    • Share target: Instagram › Share › TakeTray opens the Save reference sheet over Instagram. Done returns to Instagram; Open reel opens reel detail at its References (after onboarding, on a first launch).
    • The References sheet opens from "References · 4" on the Shooting mode card; the link badge on a reel card jumps to that reel's References.
    • The tag player and clip viewer are full-screen (no navigation bar) and always dark.
    • VN flow: Pick clips → Choose editor → Space check → Copying → Ready. "Done" returns to the reel (the steps in between are replaced, so back doesn't replay copying).

    Material 3 motion tokens. Motion explains what happened (the clip went into the reel, the next one arrived). It never decorates. With reduced motion on, slides become 150 ms fades and loops stop on their last frame.

    Foundations

    Accessibility

    Built for one-handed use in sunlight, in a moving vehicle, often with the sound off. These rules hold on every screen.

    Touch targets: at least 48 dp

    • Buttons are 40 dp tall with the touch area extended to 48 dp. Filter chips are 32 dp with a 48 dp touch area.
    • Reel chips in the tag player are 56 dp tall and half the screen wide: the most-tapped target in the app is the largest.
    • Clip tiles are about 109 × 145 dp at 360 dp width. Icon buttons are 48 × 48 dp. Adjacent targets keep at least 8 dp between them, except grid tiles (4 dp gap, but each tile is far larger than 48 dp).

    Contrast: AA in both themes

    • The token build fails if any text pair drops below 4.5:1 or any meaningful icon or border below 3:1. The lowest text pair is inversePrimary on inverseSurface in dark (5.0:1).
    • Text over video always sits on a scrim (55–70% black) or a solid pill.
    • Light-theme on-container text uses tone 10 rather than 30, for sunlight.

    Never colour alone

    • Status: icon + word in every pill.
    • Shooting mode: the word "on", the switch position and the REC dot, not just the card colour.
    • Missing clip: dashed border, icon and text.
    • Selected tile: tick and shrink, not just a tint.
    • VN space shortfall: hatched pattern plus numbers in words.

    Font scaling to 200%

    • No fixed heights on anything that holds text; rows grow.
    • At font scale 1.5 and above: reel grid goes to one column; tag player chips go to one column; clip tiles keep only the duration (the time moves to the scene header); bottom bars stack their summary above the button.
    • Navigation labels never truncate; at 2.0 they wrap to two lines.
    • Numbers use tabular figures, so counters don't jitter as they change.

    Gestures always have a button

    • Swipe between clips → arrow buttons. Long-press to select → "Select clips" in the menu. Long-press a reel chip → "Pick several" button.
    • No swipe-to-delete anywhere.
    • Horizontal swipes in the player exclude the system back-gesture edges.

    Announcements and focus

    • Snackbars are polite live regions and stay until TalkBack finishes reading them (at least 6 s with Undo).
    • "4 of 23" is announced as the clip changes.
    • Sheets and dialogs move focus to their title and return it to the control that opened them.
    • Form errors appear under the field, and focus returns to the field.

    Developer handoff

    Build notes

    Everything a developer needs to rebuild these screens in Jetpack Compose. Sizes are dp, type is sp, durations are ms. The generated theme in design/handoff/compose/ is ready to drop in.

    Flutter (the chosen stack)

    • The app is in reel-helper/app. lib/theme/tokens.g.dart is generated from these tokens (all five colour options as Material 3 ColorSchemes, type, radii, spacing, motion); lib/theme/app_theme.dart builds the ThemeData.
    • TakeTray-style widgets replace stock ones: a floating pill navigation bar (Scaffold.extendBody), pill tabs instead of FilterChip, full-bleed reel cards, the media Shooting card, floating bottom sheets (transparent sheet with a rounded, inset panel), pill dialogs and toasts.
    • Dark surfaces use surfaceContainerLowest as the page background (OLED step).
    • The Compose theme files remain in design/handoff/compose/ in case a native module is ever needed.

    Theme

    • TakeTrayTheme wraps the app; VideoTheme forces the dark scheme for the tag player and clip viewer, which sit on VideoBackground (#000).
    • Dynamic colour is off on purpose: brand colours carry meaning (marigold = waiting).
    • REC red and success green are in LocalExtendedColors.
    • Font: Plus Jakarta Sans via downloadable fonts; set fontFeatureSettings = "tnum" on durations, counts and times.

    Reference reels

    • Share target. A translucent activity with an ACTION_SEND / text/plain intent filter (excludeFromRecents, own task affinity) shows the Save reference sheet over Instagram. Done calls finish(), so the user is back in Instagram.
    • Parsing. Take the first URL in EXTRA_TEXT (Patterns.WEB_URL). Store the full link; compare without protocol, www., query (Instagram's igsh tracking) and trailing slash to detect "Already saved".
    • Sources. instagram.com (reel, post, video or profile), youtube.com / youtu.be, tiktok.com; anything else is "Link" with its host name.
    • No network. Never fetch the page, title or thumbnail. Video permission is not needed, so sharing works before onboarding.
    • Opening. Intent(ACTION_VIEW, uri); Android's app links open Instagram or YouTube when installed, otherwise the browser. On ActivityNotFoundException, open a Custom Tab. No error is shown.
    • Clipboard offer. Show "Paste link from clipboard?" only when primaryClipDescription says text (API 31+: getConfidenceScore(TextClassifier.TYPE_URL)); read the clip only when the user taps Paste, so the system "pasted" toast is expected.
    • Data. Reference(id, reelId, url, label ≤ 60, source, addedAt). Included in export/import. Deleting a reel deletes its references.
    • First launch. A reel created from a share is marked as created before onboarding; it is listed (ticked) in "Create your reels", and Open reel deep-links to it after onboarding.
